Common Questions

Admissions FAQ.

What ages does KENWIN accept?
We admit children from Nursery (around age 2) through Primary 6 (around age 11), placing each child according to age and readiness.
Is there an entrance assessment?
Yes, a short, friendly, age-appropriate interaction that helps our teachers understand your child and confirm the right class placement.
Can I transfer my child mid-session?
Yes, we accept transfer pupils throughout the year, subject to space availability. Please enquire for current openings.
Do you offer a school bus or meal plan?
Please contact the school office directly for current transport and meal plan arrangements and pricing.
