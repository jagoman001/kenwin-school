Ages 2–5
🧸

Nursery Program

Our Nursery program is built around play-based learning, the way young children learn best. Days are gentle, structured, and full of hands-on discovery.

  • ✓ Early literacy: letter sounds, phonics, and pre-reading skills
  • ✓ Early numeracy: counting, shapes, patterns, and sorting
  • ✓ Fine and gross motor skill development
  • ✓ Social skills: sharing, turn-taking, and communication
  • ✓ Creative play: art, music, storytelling, and imaginative play
Ages 6–11
📘

Primary Program

From Primary 1 through Primary 6, our curriculum builds strong core skills while nurturing curiosity, creativity, and character, preparing pupils confidently for secondary school.

  • ✓ Core subjects: English, Mathematics, Basic Science, Social Studies
  • ✓ Reading, comprehension, and creative writing
  • ✓ Creative & performing arts, and physical education
  • ✓ Values and character education woven into daily lessons
  • ✓ Regular assessments to track and support every pupil's progress
A Typical Day

Structure, with room to breathe.

1

Morning Assembly & Welcome

Pupils arrive, settle in, and start the day together with assembly.

2

Core Lessons

Focused learning blocks in literacy, numeracy, and core subjects.

3

Break & Play

Supervised outdoor play and a nutritious meal break.

4

Creative & Enrichment Time

Art, music, physical education, or storytelling sessions.

5

Wrap-Up & Dismissal

Review of the day's learning before pupils head home.
